THE PHONE, KNOT BY KNOT
SHARE THIS CHANNEL
Press and hold to light each letter. Not synced.
HOW
  1. Everyone opens the same channel and taps ARM. Phones locked upright, brightness up.
  2. Photographer: tripod, bulb, remote. Open the shutter on the long low beep after 1.
  3. Walker: stand at the first knot, screen to the lens. Step on each click.
  4. The face at the end goes on close knots, one phone-width apart (about 7 cm): shuffle sideways on the soft ticks so its parts touch.
  5. Close the shutter on the two-note end tone.
  6. The screen stays black for a few seconds after the end tone, so nothing lights the photo while the shutter closes.
  7. To leave the black screen early: hold two fingers on it for 2 seconds (solo step: one finger, 3 seconds).
