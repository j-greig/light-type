Take these quick steps to get your phone ready.

  1. DO NOT DISTURB ONA notification lights the screen and shows in the photo.
  2. BRIGHTNESS FULL, AUTO-BRIGHTNESS OFFAuto-Brightness dims the screen in the dark. iPhone: Settings › Accessibility › Display & Text Size.Phones with an LCD screen (iPhone SE, XR, 11) glow grey on black and leave a faint band: lower the brightness.
  3. TAPE OVER THE CLOCKThe clock and battery at the top stay lit and draw a line across the photo. A strip of black tape covers them.

HOW
  1. Everyone opens the same channel. The photographer sets up a tripod and a long exposure (bulb).
  2. Press START. Every phone counts 5 4 3 2 1. The photographer opens the shutter on the long low beep.
  3. Hold the phone upright, screen to the camera. On the long low beep, start walking sideways at an even pace: one tick per letter. With → walk from the camera's left to its right.
  4. With ⇄, after the three-note chime, lower the phone by its own height and walk back the other way.
  5. The photographer closes the shutter on the two-note tone. The screen stays black for 6 seconds after it.
  6. To stop early, hold three fingers on the screen for 2 seconds.

Start at ISO 200, f/8, manual focus on the walking line. If the dots bloom, turn the phone's light level down.
